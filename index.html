PROJECT KABALIKAT


PREPARED TODAY, RESILIENT TOMORROW



Welcome to Project Kabalikat, a community-driven initiative dedicated to building disaster preparedness and fostering long-term resilience. Guided by our core pillars—Kahandaan (Readiness), Bayanihan (Unity), Liderato (Leadership), and Katatagan (Strength)—our goal is to empower individuals and families with the knowledge, planning, and practical resources needed to navigate natural hazards safely and effectively.

Explore our interactive platform to access comprehensive disaster risk reduction plans, educational guides, documentary coverage, and practical family fitness routines designed to keep everyone proactive and prepared. Created by Group 1, this site serves as a vital resource hub to ensure our community stays informed, unified, and resilient for whatever tomorrow brings.
